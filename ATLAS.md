## Etiquette contract (binding for members)

The AI-PR landscape is uneven — bans at QEMU, Gentoo, NetBSD, LLVM (LLVM specifically bans AI from good-first-issues); a disclosure-standard cluster (Fedora, the kernel, ASF) uses "Assisted-by:" trailers; NVDA requires human-submitted PRs. Our rules, derived from that landscape:

1. Disclosure, always.
2. The human owns the PR.
3. Issue-first — discuss before you patch.
4. Good-first-issues are for human members.
5. No AI-generated security reports.
6. Small first PRs.

This contract is part of member onboarding.

## Vetting gates

Seven gates keep this atlas honest as it grows: live commit cadence, triage responsiveness, newcomer funnel, contribution docs, AI policy known, maintainer diversity, and mission fit. An entry that fails re-vetting moves to the dormancy list — visibly.
